# Sales-Dashboard-Supermarket-Shop-Analysis
A Power BI sales dashboard for supermarket performance analysis — featuring KPIs, category &amp; product insights, sales trends, and interactive breakdowns by channel and payment mode.

🚀 Sales Dashboard — Supermarket Shop Analysis

I created an interactive sales dashboard to analyze supermarket performance and practice end-to-end dashboard design.

🔹 Highlights:
📊 KPIs: Total Sales ($401K) 💰, Profit ($69K) 📈, Profit Margin (21%) ✅
🌍 Breakdown: Sales by Channel (Direct 🏪, Online 🌐, Wholesaler 🤝) & Payment Mode (Cash 💵 vs Online 💳)
📦 Category insights: Top Category (Category04 — $95K 🏆), Low-performing Category (Category03 — $52K ⚠)
🛒 Product insights: Top Product (Product41 — $22K ⭐)
📅 Trends: Monthly 📆 and Daily 📊 sales fluctuations with profit margin variation

🔹 Focus:
✨ Clean layout, clear KPIs, and actionable insights to help understand performance drivers and identify growth opportunities.

Excited to keep exploring data storytelling 📖 and apply these insights to real-world business contexts. Would love your feedback and suggestions! 🙌
